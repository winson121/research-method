===== PAGE 6 =====
Question 1
1 - Select a research focus:
● What specific research element would you like to focus on?
○ E.g. The usability of the interface of the current timetabling system for students switching from 
an online to on-campus study mode.
● Create 2 research questions that you would address through your research.
○ E.g. How accessible is the information required to perform the online-oncampus class switch?
○ How can we redesign the calendar view to improve the visualisation of the students’ current 
timetable?
===== PAGE 7 =====
Question 2
2 - Are there any existing data you can use to answer these 
questions?
○ E.g. system logs, timestamps, staff communication content…
===== PAGE 8 =====
Question 3
3- It is very likely that you would need to collect additional data 
to answer your questions. How would you do this?
○ E.g. Interviews, surveys, focus group, log data…
===== PAGE 9 =====
Question 4
4- Who are you going to collect this additional data from? Be very specific 
about the participant groups you collect this from
○ What criteria would you apply to select them?
○ How many participants?

Activity 2: Creating a Research Plan
Produce a plan representing the various stages of the research:
● What are the individual activities you need to conduct during each stage?
○ E.g. recruitment advertising, shortlisting participants, interview scheduling
○ Make sure to identify any dependencies between them
● What design or piloting may be required for each stage?
○ E.g. piloting a survey before you roll it out
● What key resources will be required to conduct this research?
○ E.g. $500,000; 1 year; 10 Software Engineers, 5 Research Assistants and 3 Web 
Developers…
● Timline: E.g. Gantt Chart..
● Work on the Group Flipchart

Activity 3: Resource Allocation
Management has allocated your project $3000 for 
you to outsource or contract your research.

how you might resource your research plan to 
the best of your ability.

resource everything in your original plan, so 
have a think about what to keep, what to adjust 
and what to drop.
