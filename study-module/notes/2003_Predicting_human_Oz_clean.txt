ABSTRACT

T h e study simulates a range o f possible sensors through

CONCLUSIONS 
While t h e study described here only considers a particular 
category o f office worker , a n d w e cannot y e t tell h o w well 
t h e results might translate t o other demographics , i t s results 
a r e still quite promising . W e have demonstrated that

t o achieve good results .

There a r e many areas f o r future work i n this line o f
