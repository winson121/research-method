What is research?•“… a systematic, careful enquiry or examination to discover new information or relationships and to expand/verify existing knowledge for some specified purpose.” [Bennet, 1991]•A systematic process of collecting, analyzing, and interpreting information (data) to increase understanding of a phenomenon about which we are interested or concerned. [Leedy& Ormrod, 2013]“Research is the careful search or inquiry for; endeavourto discover new facts, procedures, methods and techniques by the scientific study of a subject, course of critical investigation.” [Concise Oxford Dictionary 4th ed]

e.g. Time Dilation & Special Relativity Special relativity [Theory]“the rate of a clock C traveling between two synchronized laboratory clocks A and B, as seen by a laboratory observer, is slowed down relative to the laboratory clock rates”Muons are created by cosmic rays in the upper atmosphere of the earth.These fast moving particles (e.g. muons) should decay slower than their half-life might suggest.Rossi–Hall experiment: measured muons travelling above 0.99c (c=speed of light) at Echo Lake (3240 m) and Denver in Colorado (1616 m) [Observation]Difference in measurement at the two altitudes qualitatively confirmed the theory (i.e. less decay than the half-life predicts).IMPORTANT: prediction of a novel previously unobserved phenomena.
