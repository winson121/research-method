Before You Interview?What is your epistemological position?Epistemology: the study of what does and does not constitute your knowledge.

Before You Interview?The nature of the phenomena, or entities, to be investigated?Ontology: concepts and categories in a subject area that shows their properties and the relations between themQuestions connect to our ontological position, e.g. sexism•As a study of attitudes•As a study of discourse•As a study of institutions


===== PAGE 8 =====
Advantages Of InterviewsFlexible, in that the line of inquiry is modifiableConduct benefits from non-verbal communicationGets “behind” the immediate level of response•-Why something is said•Contrast with questionnaire based approaches
•Can be used with relatively little prior knowledge of a situation•-Typical survey method uses exploratory qualitative research•Situational, gets the participants view of the world•Engaging, often viewed positively by participants

===== PAGE 9 =====
Disadvantages Of InterviewsNot standardised-For semi-structured and unstructured interviewsNot replicable-any form of interviewTime consuming (therefore expensive)-Typically requires face-to-face meeting
Recruitment can be challenging-Time commitment on the part of participantsAnalysis take time and effort-Transcription / Qualitative analysis / Disseminating

===== PAGE 10 =====
Types Of Interview…Respondent interviews (e.g. with participant)•Structured interview•Semi-structured interviewInformer interviews (e.g. with key informant)•Unstructured interview 
Many other configurations:•Debate-based interview (i.e. adversarial)•Group-based interviews (e.g. focus group)•Multiple interviewers

===== PAGE 11 =====
Structured InterviewRigid interview styleGood when researcher has comprehensive list of questionsNo need for follow-up interviews

===== PAGE 12 =====
Semi-structured InterviewsResearcher uses interview protocol to “guide” the interviewCan incorporate conversational aspects Offers a lot of flexibility to the researcher

===== PAGE 13 =====
Unstructured InterviewsFew if any interview questions are usedProgress like a normal conversation, however, interested in research topic under reviewHelpful when researchers discussing sensitive topicsParticipant’s narratives may redirect conversation away from other aspects of the research topic
