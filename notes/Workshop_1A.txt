===== PAGE 9 =====
Introduction
This unit is all about introducing you to the types of methods that are commonly used to 
conduct research in both industry and academic settings.
Throughout the unit, we will be covering different broad types of methods.
Each week’s workshop is designed to give you a chance to practice some of these 
methods.
As this is the first week – we will start with a common scenario from an IT company, 
aiming to demonstrate that this unit will introduce real skills that you will need.
