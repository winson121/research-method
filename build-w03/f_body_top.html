ITI5125 · Surveys & Interviews — week 3

One-stop consume-mode study module for week 3: survey & questionnaire design, question wording pitfalls, sampling, the full interview craft (types, schedules, probes, focus groups, sensitive topics, conduct, transcription) and the consent process. Both workshop sheets are solved with multiple detailed model answers you can present in class, plus a full question bank and a timed simulated quiz.

The weekly quiz is multiple choice, including choose-one-or-more items. It can test concepts, case-study details (Daniela's COVID survey, the Cortana nursing-home interviews), question-wording flaws, interview types and "what would you do" role-plays. This module trains all of those formats — including the exact activity sheets your workshop groups will work through.

How this week is organised

Moodle splits week 3 into Part A: Surveys and Part B: Interviews, each with a lecture, a case study, a workshop sheet and assessed pieces that feed your assignments.

PartLearnPractice (solved in this module)
A · SurveysSurvey vs questionnaire · what surveys can capture · when questionnaires are appropriate · sampling · question forms & scales · the Daniela COVID caseWorkshop 3A: critique 10 flawed questions, then design a full questionnaire on adjusting to student life
B · InterviewsWhy interviews · epistemology & ontology · advantages/disadvantages · structured / semi-structured / unstructured · the Cortana nursing-home caseWorkshop 3B: mini-topics, a 10-minute interview schedule with probes, role-play conduct and reflections

Week's learning outcomes (from the Moodle overview): understand the purpose and role of questionnaires; identify and describe question types; design a questionnaire using best practice; know the deployment tools; understand how interviews and focus groups capture qualitative information; identify interview/focus-group types; critique interviews for good and bad practice; plan an interview appropriate for a topic; describe interview characteristics in research.

Suggested path (~2.5–3 hours)

  • Before the workshop: read the roadmap → surveys section → interviews section, doing every bite-size quiz as you go.
  • Workshop prep: study both solved workshop sections (3A and 3B) — pick the model answer set closest to your group's angle and walk in ready.
  • Assignment prep: the template/consent/transcription section if you have the interview assignment coming.
  • Last 30 minutes before the quiz: the quiz sprint section — pure rapid-recognition tables built for ~30-second questions.
  • Exam prep: work the full question bank (mixed formats), then sit the simulated quiz (randomised, timed, 20 questions).
